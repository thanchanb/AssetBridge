# Frequently Asked Questions

**Q: What is AssetBridge?**
A: AssetBridge is a privacy-critical bridging tool utilizing Zero-Knowledge proofs to shield your identity and transaction amounts when moving assets across chains.

**Q: Is it safe?**
A: We are currently in the Supermoon phase (Preprod network). While we take security very seriously, please do not use real assets on this MVP.

**Q: How do I provide feedback?**
A: You can use the built-in feedback module at the bottom of the DApp.
