# AssetBridge Brand Brief & Style Guide

AssetBridge is a secure, privacy-centric asset transfer protocol. Our visual identity reflects advanced zero-knowledge cryptography, modern cross-chain connectivity, and premium usability.

---

## 1. Visual Values
*   **Security & Anonymity**: Portrayed through shielded motifs, crescent moons, and solid border structures.
*   **Modern Cryptography**: Represented by neon accents, dark backgrounds, and subtle geometric grids.
*   **Fluid Connectivity**: Shown as arches, bridges, and smooth animated gradient transitions.

---

## 2. Core Color Palette
*   **Background (Deep Black-Violet)**: `#08060d`
*   **Accent Violet (Electric Neon)**: `#aa3bff` (HSL: `272, 100%, 62%`)
*   **Accent Cyan (Neon Light)**: `#00c2ff` (HSL: `194, 100%, 50%`)
*   **Text Principal**: `#ffffff` (High Contrast)
*   **Text Muted (Gray-Blue)**: `#a0aec0`

---

## 3. Typography
*   **Headings / Title**: `Outfit` (Futuristic geometric sans-serif)
*   **Body Copy / Interface Elements**: `Inter` or standard system sans-serif (Neutral, legible at small sizes)

---

## 4. Visual Components
*   **Logo**: Combined bridge deck with crescent moon shield node (`brand/logo.svg`).
*   **Banner**: Widescreen asset featuring left-aligned typography and right-aligned glowing bridge graphics (`brand/banner.svg`).
